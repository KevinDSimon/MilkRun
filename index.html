      Your Spectra

      Two modes

      The button with the wavy lines switches between them. The pump screen shows cycle on the left, the timer in the middle and vacuum (L) on the right.

      Letdown

      Fast, light suction to get milk flowing. Also called massage mode. The wave icon shows on screen.

      Tips

      • Use the strongest vacuum that's still comfortable, never painful. Pain can hold back letdown.
      • If flow stops early, go back to letdown mode for a minute or two to bring on another letdown, then return to expression.
      • The Spectra starts on its last settings, so turn the vacuum down before powering off.
      • The pump powers itself off after 30 minutes.
      • If the timer screen says "Screen may sleep", set Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock to Never during sessions, and turn off Low Power Mode.
      • Lean forward a little to help milk into the bottles.
